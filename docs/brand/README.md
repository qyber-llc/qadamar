# Qadamar brand prototypes

Open-source authorization by Qyber LLC. Tagline: **Every access decision. Explained.**

Open `index.html` for the English prototype gallery. `qadamar-brand-board.png` compares three directions:

1. **H Portal (recommended):** an H forming a doorway, a nod to the Hadamard gate.
2. **Q Circuit:** Qyber’s initial crossed by a circuit wire.
3. **Ket Door:** a doorway inspired by quantum ket notation.

`qadamar-mark.svg` and `qadamar-logo.svg` are the H Portal defaults. Light, dark and monochrome marks, a social preview, PNG exports and JSON design tokens are included. SVG wordmarks use system fonts; outline text before sending to print. These are prototypes, not a completed trademark or domain clearance.

The quantum reference describes the visual identity. The engine is classical and deterministic.

## Rename scope

The product name, technical identifiers (Python package, protocol URNs, Lean namespace) and brand assets all use Qadamar. Historical records and third-party notices are preserved.
